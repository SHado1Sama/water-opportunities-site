About this board

This board tracks internships, scholarships, fellowships, graduate-trainee schemes and jobs in water resources, environmental engineering, climate, sustainability, energy and waste management. Every listing is scored under published rules for field fit, eligibility for Nigeria-based students and recent graduates, and funding. The board refreshes itself daily, and a manual sweep runs twice a week.

Save the listings you like with the bookmark button, track them under Saved Opportunities, and use the Application Vault to keep your details, documents checklist and project story ready, so applying takes minutes instead of an evening of retyping.

Built and maintained by Iskeel Abdulmujeeb, water resources and environmental engineer. ✉️ iskeelabdulmujeeb18@gmail.com 💻 github.com/SHado1Sama

Always check the original listing before applying — eligibility, deadlines and locations can change, and a match score is a ranking aid, not a guarantee. Legitimate employers and scholarship bodies never charge application fees.
