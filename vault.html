How application packs work: go back to the opportunities board, open any listing and tap Application pack. The pack is built from this vault on your device: your details, your education and experience summaries, your skills, and the story framing that fits that listing, each with its own copy button, plus a checklist of documents to prepare. You still visit the employer's site and submit yourself. Nothing here submits anything for you.

The vault is a personal organiser. Always check the original listing before applying, and never pay an application fee: legitimate employers and scholarship bodies do not charge them.
